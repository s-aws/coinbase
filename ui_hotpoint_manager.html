How this works (read me first)

Hotpoint Auto-Replicate watches your fills. When an opted-in order is filled N times in the same price bucket within a rolling time window T, the engine automatically places one additional limit order at that bucket's center price.

Vocabulary

  • Bucket: a log-spaced price band of width HOTPOINT_WIDTH_PCT (e.g. 0.5%). At ~$100k BTC, one bucket is ~$500 wide. Buckets are deterministic — the same price always lands in the same bucket regardless of fill order or restart.
  • Trigger: at least HOTPOINT_TRIGGER_N fills inside one bucket within HOTPOINT_TRIGGER_WINDOW_SECONDS seconds, same product + same side, same opt-in parent.
  • Cap: at most HOTPOINT_RATE_LIMIT_N auto-placements per (product, side, bucket) per HOTPOINT_RATE_LIMIT_WINDOW_SECONDS seconds.
  • Decay: resting auto-placed orders are cancelled when their bucket goes cold (no qualifying fills in the trigger window).
  • No cascade: auto-placed rows themselves are NOT opted in, so their fills cannot trigger further auto-placements.

Opting an order in

Use the Place Test Order form below, OR set enable_hotpoint_replication=True on any insert_order_parent call in your placement code. Auto-placement happens transparently from there.

Kill switch

The big toggle at the top is a runtime kill switch — instant, no restart. The detector keeps recording fills while disabled, so when you re-enable the system already has recent context.

Liquidation risk reminder: this feature places real orders without per-order confirmation. The cap is your primary safety net (defaults to HOTPOINT_RATE_LIMIT_N=5 per 5 minutes per bucket). Watch the Recent Auto-Placements table during testing.
